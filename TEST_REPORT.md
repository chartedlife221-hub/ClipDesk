## Bug reports for remaining failures

**Bug 1: Default speaker crop ignores an off-centre subject when the face model is unavailable**

- Repro:
  1. Use an OpenCV build without `haarcascade_frontalface_default.xml`.
  2. Open `synthetic_talk_2m30` and open the 1:04.8 suggestion with *Speaker crop*.
  3. Look at the preview at 1:12.
- Expected: the guest (centre x ≈ 0.66) is framed.
- Actual: the crop is centred at x = 0.50 and the guest is cut in half. *Auto-center on face* says "face model not available…".
- Workaround: the *Horizontal position* slider (0.655 framed him fully).
- Fix candidates:
  - with requirements installed, auto-centre should work (verify on your machine)
  - default to the previous clip's position for the same source

**Bug 2: Manual boundary edits are not snapped to word or sentence edges**

- Repro:
  1. Create a clip at 1:24–1:46 on `synthetic_talk_2m30`.
  2. Drag the start handle right and the end handle left (→ 1:25.8–1:43.5).
  3. Export.
- Expected: no word cut off at either edge.
- Actual: starts mid-sentence ("…embarrassed…" at -53 dB in the first 1.5 s), ends inside the host's question (-33 dB until the last frame).
- Discovered clips are snapped; manual edits are kept exactly as set.
- Fix candidate: a "snap to nearest word gap" toggle (needs your preference: exact vs snapped).
